Services

What I do

Residential

Rekeying, new deadbolts, broken-key extraction, and lockouts handled at your door. Whether you're moving in or locked out, we make your home yours again.

Commercial

Rekeying, lock repair and replacement, lockouts, and commercial-grade hardware installed on-site. When a key walks off or a lock gives out, we get you back in and secure, quickly.

Master Key Systems

One key for the owner, the right keys for everyone else. I design and pin master key systems so each person opens only the doors they should, and I keep the records so adding or changing a key later is simple.

Rentals & Property Management

Fast rekeys between tenants, consistent pricing, and one locksmith who knows your properties. Built for landlords and property managers.

Key Duplication

Clean, accurate copies cut on the spot. House keys, office keys, and spares, done right the first time.

When to call

Good times to call a locksmith

  • You just bought a home. You don't know who else has a key.
  • A tenant moved out. Rekey before the next one moves in.
  • An employee left. Their key may not have left with them.
  • A key broke off in the lock. Don't force it, call.
  • A lock is sticking or failing. Fix it before it quits.
  • You're locked out. Home or business, we'll get you in.
